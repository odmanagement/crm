1 — Engagement and Services

(a) Engagement. The Client hereby engages the Consultant to provide and perform the financial advisor services in respect of the application for [SME Loan / Personal Loan / Credit Card / Housing Loan ("Finance Product")] ("Services") and the Consultant hereby accepts the engagement.

(b) Standard of Services. All Services to be provided by Consultant shall be performed with promptness and diligence in a workmanlike manner and at a level of proficiency to be expected of a consultant with the background and experience that Consultant has represented it has. The Client shall provide such access to its information, property and personnel as may be reasonably required in order to permit the Consultant to perform the Services.

2 — Consultancy Fee and Payment

(a) Consultancy Fee. In consideration of the Services to be rendered hereunder, the Client shall pay Consultant a Consultancy Fee at the rate stipulated in Clause 9 hereof for Services provided.

(b) Payment. The Consultant shall submit to the Client a single invoice detailing the Services performed or to be performed and the amount of the Consultancy Fee due. Invoices in respect of the Services rendered will be issued on the date of this Agreement or upon the Client's receipt of the Finance Product. All such amounts of Consultancy Fee stated in the invoices shall be due and payable within THREE (3) working days from the date of disbursement of loan by bank, the approval of the credit card, or the Client receiving its notices thereof, whichever is earlier, failing which the Client shall be liable to pay interest on the unpaid amount at the rate of ten percent (10%) per annum, calculated from the day immediately after the expiry of the foregoing THREE (3) working days to the date of payment, based on a 365-day year on the actual number of days elapsed, accruing from day to day.

3 — Work Product and License

(a) Defined. "Work Product" means all work product generated by Consultant solely or jointly with others in the performance of the Services, including but not limited to any and all information, notes, material, drawings, records, diagrams, formulae, processes, technology, firmware, software, know-how, designs, ideas, discoveries, inventions, improvements, copyrights, trademarks and trade secrets.

(b) Ownership. Consultant shall be entitled to all right, title and interest in and to the Work Product, which shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Consultant. The Client shall have no rights of any kind in such Work Product and will not make any use of it without the Consultant's prior written consent.

(c) License. Where Consultant integrates previously created work into any Work Product, the Consultant may license the Client to use the Work Product subject to terms to be agreed between the parties.

4 — Confidential Information

(a) Defined. "Confidential Information" means the Work Product and any and all information relating to the Client's business, disclosed either directly or indirectly to Consultant, excluding information that (i) is or comes into the public domain without breach of this Agreement, (ii) was in the Consultant's possession prior to receipt from the Client, (iii) is acquired from a third party not under an obligation of confidentiality, or (iv) is independently developed by the Consultant without use of the Client's Confidential Information.

(b) Obligations of Non-Disclosure and Non-Use. Unless otherwise agreed in advance in writing by the Client, Consultant will not, except as required by law or court order, use the Confidential Information for any purpose other than performance of the Services, nor disclose it to any third party save to employees who need to know and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

(c) Return. On termination or expiry, or at the Client's earlier request, Consultant will deliver to the Client all Confidential Information in tangible form, retaining one copy for its legal files.

The Consultant agrees that all Services are rendered as an independent contractor and that this Agreement does not create an employer-employee relationship. The Consultant has no right to employee benefits, agrees to pay all taxes due on the Consultancy Fee, and is not authorised to act as the Client's agent or make commitments on its behalf.

Each party agrees not to disclose the existence or contents of this Agreement to any third party without the other's prior written consent, except to advisers/auditors who need to know, as required by law or court order, in connection with a reorganisation or merger, or as required to enforce this Agreement.
